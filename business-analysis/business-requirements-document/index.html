Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Professional Business Analysis Template

The Business Requirements Document (BRD) is the foundation of a successful project. It captures the business need, defines project objectives, establishes scope, identifies stakeholders, and documents the high-level business and functional requirements that guide solution design and implementation.

This template follows industry best practices and can be adapted for Oracle Cloud ERP implementations, Oracle APEX development, business process improvements, systems integrations, reporting initiatives, and digital transformation projects.

Typical Use Cases

  • Oracle Cloud ERP Implementations
  • Business Process Transformation
  • Oracle APEX Projects
  • System Integrations
  • Reporting & Analytics Initiatives
  • Application Enhancements
  • Digital Transformation Programs
